# Solution-page background images

The two decorative images in `public/images/solutions/` were created with the
built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 15 September 2026 and encoded as WebP
for the website. They depict generic infrastructure, not documented AST client
installations. The original page copy is unchanged.

- `telecommunications-infrastructure.webp`: communications mast with microwave dishes.
- `security-infrastructure.webp`: industrial perimeter security camera.

Both files are shipped locally and require no external image host. Dark CSS
gradients preserve heading readability. The images are decorative and have empty
alternative text. Other existing image references elsewhere in the site remain
as specified in the source, including external Unsplash references.

## Generation prompts

### Telecommunications

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative background photograph for a dark corporate telecommunications solutions website. Create a wide landscape 16:9 editorial-style photograph of a modern communications mast with realistic round microwave dishes and slender panel antennas, seen at medium distance on a low arid ridge at blue hour. The mast and dishes occupy the RIGHT third and are clearly recognizable with subtle cool rim lighting; the LEFT half is calm dark charcoal sky and distant low hills, deliberately uncluttered to sit behind large white page headings. Restrained slate and muted steel blue palette, natural atmospheric depth, realistic engineering hardware, elegant understated infrastructure photography. Retain some visible detail and midtones on the right because the website adds dark gradients. No people, weapons, flags, labels, logos, lettering, watermarks, UI or drawn signal lines. This is illustrative generic infrastructure, not a named real client installation.

### Security

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative background photograph for a dark corporate security solutions website, matching understated communications infrastructure photography. Create a wide landscape 16:9 editorial-style photograph of a modern weatherproof security camera on a clean metal mounting arm at the edge of an industrial facility, seen from a low three-quarter angle. Main camera occupies the upper RIGHT third, pointing slightly left/down across a quiet secure perimeter; distant fencing and industrial architectural lines dissolve into softly defocused blue-hour background. The LEFT half has uncluttered dark charcoal negative space for large white page headings. Restrained slate and muted steel blue palette, soft practical light, tactile realistic metal housing, low-key but visible midtone detail on the camera because the website will add dark gradients. Understated and credible, no exaggerated sci-fi surveillance, no people, no screens, no labels, logos, text, watermarks, UI, weapons or flags. Generic illustrative facility, not a named real client installation.
